Gavel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Gavel helps a Toastmasters club run its meetings together: planning the agenda, timing speakers, counting filler words, noting grammar, and keeping a record of it all. This policy explains what Gavel keeps about you, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Who runs Gavel: kaarthikeyan, kaarthik2504@gmail.com.

What Gavel keeps

Your account

  • Your email address and the name you show to your club.
  • Your password. It is never stored as written: our sign-in provider keeps only a salted hash of it, which cannot be turned back into the password.

Your club

  • Which club you belong to, and your role in it (owner, admin or member; a Toastmaster or a guest).
  • Invite codes an admin creates, and a log of admin actions — who changed whose role, who started or finished a meeting — so a club can see how its roster changed.

Your meetings

  • Meetings your club plans, the agenda, and who holds each part.
  • What was recorded while you spoke: your speaking time and whether it was within the lights, the filler words the Ah-Counter heard, and the Grammarian's notes, including how often you used the word of the day.

Notifications

  • If you allow notifications, a token that lets us reach your phone, and which kind of phone it is (Android or iPhone).
  • A copy of each notification sent to you.

On your phone only

  • Your sign-in, your settings, a copy of your club's recent meetings so the app works without signal, and anything you recorded that has not reached the club yet. All of this is removed from the phone when you sign out.

Gavel does not use advertising, analytics or tracking, does not read your contacts, location, photos or files, and does not sell or share your information with anyone for their own use.

Who can see it

  • Your club sees the roster, the meetings, and what was recorded in them. A club's record is shared with everyone in that club, and with nobody else.
  • Your progress — the statistics built from your turns across meetings — is visible to you and to your club's owner and admins, not to other members.
  • Nobody outside your club can see anything about it. The server enforces this for every request; it is not left to the app.

Keeping and deleting it

Your information is kept while you have an account.

To delete your account: open Settings → Delete my account. This removes, for good: your name, email, sign-in, place on the roster, notification tokens and notification history.

Meetings you took part in still happened, so the club keeps its record of them: your turns stay, credited to "Former member" and no longer linked to you.

If you own a club, hand it to another member first (in Your club), so it is not left without an owner. If you are the only person left in it, the club and its meetings are deleted with your account.

If you can no longer open the app, email kaarthik2504@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you within 30 days.

Children

Gavel is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly keep information about them. If you believe a child has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.
